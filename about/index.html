Who We Are

Easy Connect Solutions is a Shah Alam-based qualified IT hardware supplier and IT Asset Disposition (ITAD) service specialist. We provide rigorously tested, Grade A refurbished laptops, desktops, workstations and peripherals.

Every device we supply passes our 24-point verified audit: military-grade data wiping, MemTest86 diagnostics, physical restoration and custom OS configuration before deployment. When it leaves our facility, it performs like new, backed by a full warranty and dedicated expert support, helping organizations reduce IT costs while supporting sustainability goals.

Vision, Mission & Values

Leading Malaysia's circular technology economy, where premium IT hardware meets sustainable business practices.

Our Vision

Building a Smarter Circular Technology Future

To lead Southeast Asia's transition toward a circular technology economy, where enterprise IT assets are continuously reused, refurbished and repurposed to maximize value, reduce e-waste and support sustainable transformation for businesses and institutions.

Our Mission

Empowering Organizations Through Sustainable Technology

To deliver reliable, enterprise-grade technology solutions through extending the lifecycle of technology assets, protecting data integrity and supporting circular economy principles responsible for e-waste management.
