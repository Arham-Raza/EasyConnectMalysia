Institution Bulk Orders
Education Grade

Educational institutions face a recurring challenge: equipping classrooms, labs and students with reliable and capable devices while respecting constrained technology budgets. A 5-year-old business laptop from brands like Dell, HP, or Lenovo, when Grade A refurbished and professionally restored, delivers the performance students need for coursework, research, coding and video conferencing at a fraction of new device cost.

We supply bulk education laptops to schools, colleges, universities and training centres across Malaysia with refurbished hardware and managed Chromebook fleets.

Why Malaysian Educational Institutions Choose Refurbished

Budget Efficiency

Device budgets in education are constrained. Grade A refurbished laptops offer reliable quality at 30-40% reduced costs relative to new devices that provide the same specifications. This provides extra money for software licensing, networking or teacher training.

Consistent Classroom Specification

When it comes to equipping a lab or installing a technological system in a classroom, consistent specifications across all devices are essential. We set up computer labs where each computer has the same memory, storage and processing power.

Managed Chromebook Fleets for Google Workspace

Managed Chromebooks work best for schools that are utilizing Google Classroom and Google Workspace for Education. We prepare the devices according to your Google Admin Console domain prior to shipment, meaning that the device is all set once it arrives at your school.

Institutional Procurement Support

We know the procurement process of the education sector and provide formal quotations for finance committee review. We help with the ordering process, and deliveries are coordinated according to the school calendar.
