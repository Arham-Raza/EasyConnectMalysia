# Easy Connect Solutions Malaysia - LLM Context

## About
Easy Connect Solutions is an IT hardware supplier and technology lifecycle services provider based in Shah Alam, Selangor, Malaysia.

## Products
- Refurbished business laptops: Dell Latitude, HP EliteBook and Lenovo ThinkPad
- Student and institutional laptops, including selected Chromebooks
- Professional workstations: HP Z-Series and Dell Precision
- Gaming laptops: ASUS ROG, Acer Nitro, MSI and Lenovo Legion
- Apple MacBook Pro and iMac devices for creative workflows
- Desktops, monitors and computer accessories

## Solutions
- IT asset disposition and secure data handling
- Responsible e-waste management and sustainability support
- Short- and long-term IT equipment rental
- Bulk hardware procurement and fleet configuration
- IT asset buyback and value recovery

Specific standards, certificates, warranty terms, turnaround times, savings figures and stock availability depend on the written quotation and confirmed service scope. They must not be inferred from this summary.

## Coverage
Easy Connect Solutions serves organizations across Malaysia. Delivery, collection and on-site availability depend on location and project scope.
