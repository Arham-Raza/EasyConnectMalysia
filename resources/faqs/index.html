Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about buying certified renewed IT hardware from Easy Connect Solutions Malaysia.

About Refurbished IT

A certified renewed device is one that has been returned to working condition through a structured process — data wiping, hardware testing and diagnostics, physical cleaning, component repair or replacement where necessary, and fresh OS installation. At Easy Connect, every device passes our 24-point audit before it's listed for sale. "Renewed" and "refurbished" mean the same thing; we use both interchangeably.

Business-class devices are designed for managed professional environments and commonly offer durable construction, serviceable components and stronger security features. Each available unit should still be assessed by its model, age, specification and condition.

Grades describe cosmetic and condition expectations, but the exact definition used for a quotation is disclosed with the product offer. See our Refurbishment Policy for the general assessment principles.

We source exclusively from tier-1 Malaysian and regional corporations undergoing IT fleet upgrades, certified ITAD partners with documented chain of custody, and government or institutional tenders with verifiable asset history. We do not purchase hardware from unknown or unverified sources. Every device arrives with a documented serial number and is processed through our facility before sale.

Warranty & Support

All devices come with a minimum 6-month hardware warranty. Qualifying products carry up to a 12-month warranty. The warranty covers hardware component failures, functional performance issues, and software configuration problems arising from our pre-delivery setup. It does not cover physical damage, liquid damage, or software issues from third-party installations after delivery. See our Terms & Conditions for full warranty details.

Contact us via WhatsApp at +6011 2090 6561 or email at info@easyconnect.my. We will assess the issue and either guide you through a software fix remotely, arrange collection for repair, or provide a swap unit (for corporate clients with active fleet agreements). Warranty repairs are completed at no charge. Corporate clients receive a replacement unit within 48 hours while their device is being assessed.

Batteries are consumable components. Available battery-health information, exclusions and any applicable initial-failure coverage are disclosed in the product quotation and warranty terms.

Data Security

Previous data is removed using the processing method agreed for the device or project. Organizations with specific sanitization or reporting requirements should state them before quotation so the correct scope can be confirmed.

Certificate and reporting availability depends on the processing scope agreed for the specific device or ITAD project. Contact us before ordering if this documentation is required.
