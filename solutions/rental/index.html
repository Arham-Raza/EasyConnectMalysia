Corporate Laptop, Computer & IT Equipment Rental in Malaysia

Whether you need 10 laptops for a one-day training session, 200 computers for an examination centre, or a multi-year corporate IT leasing program, Easy Connect provides flexible rental solutions tailored to your organization.

FLEXIBLE LEASING

We supply professionally refurbished business-grade laptops, desktops, workstations, monitors and accessories, fully configured to your specifications before delivery anywhere in Malaysia. Every rental includes deployment support, technical assistance throughout the rental period, secure collection and certified data sanitization through our authorized ITAD partners before devices are refurbished, remarketed or responsibly recycled.

Instead of investing heavily in hardware that may only be needed temporarily, organizations can reduce upfront capital expenditure while supporting a more sustainable circular IT lifecycle through IT equipment rental.

Why Rent Instead of Buying?

Buying hardware makes sense when equipment will remain in service for many years. Rental is often the better option when organizations need flexibility.

Benefits include:

  • Lower upfront investment
  • Preserve cash flow
  • Convert CapEx into predictable OpEx
  • Scale devices up or down as projects change
  • No storage of unused equipment
  • No end-of-life disposal management
  • Latest business-grade hardware
  • Sustainable IT lifecycle management

Laptop & Computer Rental Solutions for Every Business Requirement

Event & Short-Term Laptop Rental (1 Day - 3 Months)

Ideal for:

  • Corporate training
  • Conferences
  • Product launches
  • Examination centres
  • Temporary offices
  • Seasonal workforce expansion

Our business laptops are configured to your requirements before dispatch. Where possible, we can pre-install your required operating system, software, user accounts and network settings, helping reduce deployment time and ensuring devices are ready for use upon delivery.

Project-Based Computer Rental (3 - 12 Months)

Perfect for organizations expanding project teams, contractors, consultants, software development, audits, or temporary operations.

Instead of purchasing new hardware, rent devices only for the required duration. Once the project ends, equipment is collected, securely data-wiped through our certified ITAD partners, and prepared for its next lifecycle through refurbishment or responsible recycling.

Corporate Laptop Leasing & IT Fleet Rental (1-3 Years)

For organizations looking to convert hardware investment from CapEx to OpEx, long-term leasing provides predictable monthly costs without managing depreciation or asset disposal. You can also trade in retired devices through our IT asset buyback service to offset leasing transition costs. Fleet leasing includes:

  • Custom hardware specifications
  • RAM & SSD upgrades
  • Software installation (where applicable)
  • Windows deployment
  • Asset tagging
  • BIOS configuration
  • Corporate imaging
  • Scheduled refresh programs

Our Rental Process: From Request to Return

01

Rental Enquiry

Submit requirements via contact form, email, or WhatsApp: device type/specification, quantity, duration, delivery location, configuration requirements. We respond within one business day with availability and pricing.

02

Device Configuration

Every laptop or desktop is prepared according to your requirements before dispatch. Software installation wherever possible, operating system deployment, security settings, network configuration, asset tagging and hardware upgrades are completed prior to delivery so equipment is ready to use immediately.

03

Nationwide Delivery

Tracked delivery to your premises, event venue, or multiple locations across Peninsular Malaysia. For large orders, a coordinated delivery schedule is provided with tracking references.

04

On-Call Support

Our technical team is available throughout the rental period. Hardware failures are resolved within 24 hours; a replacement unit is dispatched, and the failed unit is collected. For on-site event support, technical staff is available where required.

05

Collection, Data Wiping & Responsible IT Lifecycle

At the end of the rental period, equipment is collected from your location and devices undergo certified data sanitization by our authorized ITAD partners, with certificates of data destruction available upon request. Depending on condition, equipment is then:

  • professionally refurbished for reuse,
  • remarketed,
  • or its useful parts are recycled through licensed e-waste recycling partners.
