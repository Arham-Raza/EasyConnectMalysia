We supply refurbished and new business laptops, desktops, workstations, monitors and accessories from leading brands including Dell, HP, Lenovo, Acer, Apple and more. Why Organizations Choose Us for Bulk IT Procurement

Consistent Hardware Specifications Across Every Deployment

Managing hundreds of devices becomes significantly easier when every unit shares the same hardware profile. We source equipment to a consistent specification wherever possible, helping simplify:

  • Deployment and configuration
  • Software compatibility
  • Asset management and tracking
  • Long-term support and maintenance

This creates a more predictable IT environment and makes managing large-scale deployments easier for your team.

Custom Configuration Before Delivery

Every deployment can be prepared to suit your IT environment before shipment. Where possible, we can configure:

  • Windows deployment
  • Operating system installation
  • Required software pre-installation
  • Domain joining
  • Microsoft Intune enrolment
  • Asset tagging
  • BIOS configuration
  • User account creation
  • Network settings
  • RAM and SSD upgrades

Devices arrive ready for deployment, reducing workload for your internal IT team.

Buy New or Refurbished Business IT Equipment

Every organization has different budget, performance and sustainability requirements. We can supply:

  • Grade A refurbished business laptops
  • New laptops and desktops
  • Business workstations
  • Monitors and accessories
  • Mixed fleet deployments

Our team helps determine the most cost-effective solution based on your project, lifecycle expectations and available budget.

Complete Fleet Replacement & Responsible Asset Retirement

A fleet refresh often involves replacing existing equipment as well as deploying new devices. Instead of coordinating multiple vendors, we can help manage the entire transition through our wider IT lifecycle services.

Existing hardware can be:

  • securely collected
  • processed through authorized third-party ITAD partners
  • certified data wiped
  • refurbished for reuse
  • remarketed where appropriate
  • recycled through licensed e-waste recovery partners if no longer reusable

Certificates of Data Destruction and chain-of-custody documentation are available upon request.

How Fleet Procurement Works - We Handle Everything

From specification discussion to deployed devices, we manage the entire process. Here is what a typical engagement looks like:

01

Requirements Briefing

Your IT manager, procurement officer, or finance administrator defines exact specifications: CPU generation, RAM, storage, display size, OS version, any pre-installed software, domain join requirements and delivery timeline.

02

Stock Verification

We confirm availability from our sourcing network. For large orders (200+ units), we provide lead times and phased delivery schedules.

03

Formal Quotation

Written quotation referencing specification, unit pricing, quantity discount (if applicable), delivery terms, configuration options and warranty coverage.

04

Audit & Configuration

Every unit passes the 24-point audit. Devices are configured to specification: OS installation, software pre-load (where applicable) and asset tagging with your organizational prefix.

05

Nationwide Delivery

Tracked logistics to your premises or multiple locations across Peninsular Malaysia. Proof of delivery provided. On-site deployment assistance available for large fleet rollouts.

06

Warranty & Support

Single point of contact for all warranty claims. 72-hour swap guarantee for corporate clients. Hardware issues resolved with replacement dispatch before faulty unit retrieval.
