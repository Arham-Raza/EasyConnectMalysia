How Bulk Procurement Works

01

Specification Discussion

We work with your IT manager or procurement team to define the exact specification — model, CPU, RAM, storage, OS version, and any software pre-installed.

02

Sourcing & Availability Check

We confirm stock availability from our network of sourcing partners. For large orders, we provide a lead time and phased delivery schedule if required.

03

Audit & Configuration

Every unit passes our 24-point audit. Devices are configured to your spec — OS installation, software, network settings, domain join, asset tagging.

04

Delivery & Deployment

Phased or single delivery to your premises or multiple locations. Tracked logistics with proof of delivery. On-site deployment assistance available on request.

05

Old Fleet Retirement

Old-fleet collection can be coordinated through a separate ITAD scope. Data-handling methods and supporting records are defined before the engagement begins.

06

Ongoing Support

A single account contact coordinates approved warranty claims, replacement requests and technical support according to the terms in the quotation.
